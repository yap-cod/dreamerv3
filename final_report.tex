\begin{abstract}
Model-based agents such as DreamerV3~\cite{hafner2023dreamerv3} optimize policies on imagined rollouts, so biases in predicted returns can distort learning. On Crafter~\cite{hafner2021crafter}, we inject a \emph{controlled} wood-collection bonus \(\epsilon\in\{0,1,3,5\}\) into the scalar training signal used by RSSM gradients, producing proxy skew and---at high \(\epsilon\)---patterns consistent with exploitation of imperfect proxy returns. Our core technical contribution remains \textbf{MO-Dreamer}: a vectorized reward decomposition with Cobb--Douglas actor scalarization, plus a \textbf{dynamic health-reweighting} variant. We extend the original plan with a \textbf{scalar oracle baseline} that adds an achievement-based preference term (\(\alpha{=}0.3\)) without changing network structure~\cite{christiano2017rlhf}. To avoid comparing incompatible training scores across families, we report \emph{native} Crafter telemetry (summed environment reward, health) in stratified plots, and quantify an \emph{operational proxy--true gap} \(\Delta\) between logged episode scores and native reward aggregates. Across completed single-seed runs (\(\approx\)1M steps where indicated), mitigation is strongest for dynamic MO at high \(\epsilon\); oracle shaping preserves competitive native traces at \(\epsilon{=}1\) but induces large \(\Delta\) (expected, because \(\tilde r\) includes components absent from native reward sums).
\end{abstract}

%------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Problem}
Reward misspecification and proxy overoptimization~\cite{gao2022scaling} are recurring themes when learned agents optimize surrogates rather than latent intent. In Dreamer-class agents~\cite{hafner2023dreamerv3}, policy gradients flow through imagined trajectories conditioned on a predicted reward channel. If training rewards inflate particular behaviors (\emph{e.g.,} crafting loops), gradients can amplify policies that excel under the surrogate while deviating along dimensions that Crafter natives care about (health, breadth of achievement progression).

\textbf{Intellectual contributions.} (i)~A reproducible Crafter \(\times\) DreamerV3 testbed for wood-targeted misspecification. (ii)~MO-Dreamer with Cobb--Douglas scalarization and a dynamic \(\omega_{\mathrm{hp}}\) variant (Phase~3A/3B). (iii)~A lightweight scalar \emph{preference oracle} baseline (Phase~3C). (iv)~A measurement protocol separating \emph{native} aggregates from trainer episode scores plus a documented \(\Delta\) diagnostic.

\textbf{Hypotheses (tested as in the project proposal, with final epistemic status).}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{H\arabic*:}, leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textit{H1 (misspecification signature).} Increasing \(\epsilon\) shifts native health and/or score dynamics relative to clean training; high-\(\epsilon\) scalar runs show early proxy gains and late-stage degradation or instability.
  \item \textit{H2 (mitigation vs.\ scalar).} MO variants reduce proxy--true pressure or improve native outcome traces relative to scalar Dreamer at matched \(\epsilon\), with performance degrading at large \(\epsilon\) for static weights.
  \item \textit{H3 (architecture vs.\ scalar shaping).} Oracle shaping provides an architecture-agnostic intervention; its effect should be evaluated on \emph{native} panels and not solely on \(\Delta\), because \(\tilde r\) embeds terms outside native Crafter sums.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{How we test them.} Systematic sweeps over \(\epsilon\) within each architecture family, plus oracle \((\epsilon,\alpha)\) pairs, with logging into JSONL; figures stratify families; Table~\ref{tab:gap} summarizes tail \(\Delta\).

\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{World models and Dreamer.} DreamerV3 learns an RSSM and trains actor--critic objectives on imagined futures~\cite{hafner2023dreamerv3}. Our study is not a scaling paper; we interrogate \emph{reward channel design} inside that loop.

\paragraph{Reward hacking and overoptimization.} Proxy gaming under misspecified or learned reward is standard in alignment discussions~\cite{gao2022scaling}. RL from human preferences~\cite{christiano2017rlhf} motivates our oracle as a toy ``preference channel'' on scalar returns.

\paragraph{Multi-objective RL.} Product-form or non-linear scalarizations can restrict pure trade-off corners relative to linear weights; we instantiate this idea via Cobb--Douglas advantages on vector value predictions.

\paragraph{Crafter.} Crafter~\cite{hafner2021crafter} couples survival, achievements, and diverse skills; we leverage its logging to obtain native reward sums orthogonal to hacked training tensors.

\section{Problem formulation}
\subsection{Controlled surrogate}
Let \(r^{\mathrm{C}}_t\) denote Crafter's native scalar reward signal. Training uses a modified surrogate \(r^{\epsilon}_t = r^{\mathrm{C}}_t + \epsilon\,\mathbf{1}[\mathrm{wood\ up}]\).

\subsection{Operational gap}
Let \(S^{\mathrm{log}}_k\) be the trainer's episodic statistic \texttt{episode/score} at reporting step \(k\), and \(R^{\mathrm{nat}}_k\) the episodic aggregate \texttt{epstats/log/reward/sum} extracted from Crafter inventory logs \emph{without} augmentation. Sparse rows are merged (outer join + light forward-fill) so curves share an environment-step axis after smoothing. Define
\begin{equation}
\Delta_k = S^{\mathrm{log}}_k - R^{\mathrm{nat}}_k .
\end{equation}
\(\Delta\) measures \emph{surrogate divergence from native aggregates at the episodic-reporting granularity}; it does \emph{not} imply a unique MDP Bellman residual.

\subsection{Risk \& scope.}
We distinguish (i)~dense wood bonus pathology, from (ii)~errors exclusively attributable to imaginary dynamics. Dreamer gradients use learned rewards \(f_\psi(z)\): our \(\Delta\) complements but does not replace model-based hallucination assays.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Phase 1: Scalar DreamerV3}
Default DreamerV3 with surrogate \(r^{\epsilon}_t\) injected in the Crafter wrapper.

\subsection{Phase 3A/3B: MO-Dreamer}
We expose a vector reward \(\vec{R}_t=[R^{\mathrm{hack}}_t,\,\Delta hp_t,\,\Delta ach_t]\) and extend reward / value heads to \(\mathbb{R}^3\). The actor optimizes Cobb--Douglas utility (check-in formulation):
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{actor}} = -\mathbb{E}\!\left[\prod_{i=1}^3\left(\mathrm{symexp}(\hat{V}_{\xi,i})\right)^{\omega_i}\right] .
\label{eq:cobb_douglas}
\end{equation}

Static MO uses \(\omega_i=1/3\). Dynamic MO increases \(\omega_{\mathrm{hp}}\) when health declines (homeostatic schedule as in proposal; \(\beta\) tuned in codebase):
\begin{equation}
\omega_{\mathrm{hp}}(h_t)=\tfrac13+\beta\left(1-\tfrac{h_t}{h_{\max}}\right),
\label{eq:dynamic_weight}
\end{equation}
with renormalization.

\subsection{Phase 3C: Oracle scalar shaping}
Architecture unchanged; scalar return becomes
\begin{equation}
\tilde{r}_t = r^{\epsilon}_t+\alpha\cdot \Delta\mathrm{ach}_t ,\quad \alpha=0.3,
\end{equation}
with runs at \(\epsilon\in\{1,5\}\).

\subsection{Implementation.}
Modifications span \texttt{embodied/envs/crafter.py} (bonus, vectorization, oracle), \texttt{dreamerv3/agent.py} (scalar vs.\ vector \(\lambda\)-returns / bootstrapping), and Colab launchers. Figures generated by \texttt{plot\_final\_figures.py}.

\begin{remark}[Oracle \(\Delta\) inflation]
\label{rem:oracle_delta}
Because \(\tilde r\) contains achievement shaping absent from Crafter-native sums, large oracle \(\Delta\) is mechanically expected---it signals \emph{surrogate augmentation}, not necessarily worse native outcomes.
\end{remark}

\section{Results and analysis}
\textbf{Phase 1 (scalar).} Higher \(\epsilon\) bends native reward and health traces away from the clean row (Fig.~\ref{fig:outcomes_cat}, row~1): proxy-skew concentrates on wood-linked channels while episodic morphology compresses at large \(\epsilon\) (cf.\ check-in ``self-undermining'' score pattern).

\textbf{Static vs.\ dynamic MO.} Cobb--Douglas static weights give partial robustness (\(\epsilon{=}1,3\) in check-in curves). Dynamic homeostasis delays deterioration at \(\epsilon{=}5\) in long-horizon health traces (check-in qualitative claim). Gap table: Dynamic \(\epsilon{=}5\) mean \(\Delta{=}15.69\) sits below Scalar \(19.28\) (\(\approx 18{,}\%\) relative reduction)--evidence bearing on H2, though we did \emph{not} enforce the project's informal ``\(20{\%}\)'' threshold uniformly across metrics.

\textbf{Oracle.} \(\epsilon{=}1\) oracle tracks favorable native telemetry (Fig.~\ref{fig:outcomes_cat}, row~4 left/right) comparable to strong baselines, supporting H3 partly: shaping can substitute for architectural change at moderate perturbation. \(\epsilon{=}5\) remains challenging; \(\Delta\) is large by construction (Remark~\ref{rem:oracle_delta}).

\subsection{Emergent patterns (cf.\ midpoint check-in)}
\paragraph{Self-undermining proxies.} Aggressive wood chasing can shorten effective horizon, so late episodic scores under \(\epsilon{=}5\) need not dominate the clean baseline even while the surrogate remains inflated---a cautionary instance of local proxy improvement without global return lift.
\paragraph{Magnitude cliffs in static MO.} Equal Cobb--Douglas weights provide partial decoupling yet still admit log-magnitude dominance once one channel dwarfs others; dynamic health reweighting delays but does not erase that ceiling in our regime.

\section{Discussion}
\paragraph{Hypothesis audit.}
\textit{H1:} broadly supported qualitatively (native divergence under hack). \(\Delta\) rises with \(\epsilon\) in Phase~1 tails but non-monotonicity appears at \(\epsilon{=}5\) vs.\ \(\epsilon{=}3\) owing to collapsing episode morphology---we report numbers rather than assert strict monotonicity.
\textit{H2:} partially supported via MO gap reductions (\(\epsilon{=}5\) dynamic vs scalar) plus native-health narratives from check-ins.
\textit{H3:} updated---oracle existed at submission; judgments must pair native curves with surrogate gap semantics.

\paragraph{Difficulties (per syllabus).}
Engineering brittle shape bugs when toggling oracle scalars (\texttt{replay} bootstrap ranks), reconciling disparate JSONL densities, constrained Colab turnaround, incomplete multi-seeding.

\paragraph{Lessons.}
(i)~Always surface \emph{native} reward before cross-architecture leaderboard comparisons. (ii)~Product utilities buy time but exhibit magnitude cliffs. (iii)~Operational \(\Delta\) is valuable if narrated narrowly.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Multi-seeds; ensemble-based pessimistic imagination; shortened Dreamer horizons as a causal lever; full preference-data oracle versus achievement proxy.

\section{Conclusion}
We positioned imagination-side reward hacking in DreamerV3 as an alignment-relevant surrogate-control problem on Crafter, implemented MO mitigation lines and oracle shaping, and delivered a reproducible telemetry + \(\Delta\) analysis layer. Positive results are nuanced: mitigations alter native and surrogate traces in structured ways rather than guaranteeing monotonic superiority.
